Riverton launched its bike-share program in 2019 with 300 bikes and 25 docking stations. By 2023 the fleet had grown to 1,150 bikes and 90 stations. A single ride costs 2 dollars for the first 30 minutes, and each additional 15 minutes costs 1 dollar. Monthly passes cost 20 dollars and include unlimited rides under 45 minutes. The program is run by the Riverton Transit Authority, which reported 410,000 rides in 2023. Riders must be at least 16 years old, and helmets are recommended but not required. Bikes are not available between midnight and 5 a.m. because the docking stations are closed for maintenance.
