The Harbor Science Museum opened in 1987 and now welcomes about 600,000 visitors each year. General admission is 18 dollars for adults and 12 dollars for children aged 5 to 12, while children under 5 enter free. The planetarium shows run every hour from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. and require a separate 6 dollar ticket. The museum's largest exhibit is a 24 meter blue whale skeleton that hangs in the main hall. Members receive free admission all year and a 10 percent discount at the gift shop. Photography is allowed in all galleries, but flash photography is prohibited in the planetarium. The museum is open Tuesday through Sunday and is closed on Mondays.
