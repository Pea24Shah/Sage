Alder Library cardholders may borrow up to 10 items at a time. Books are loaned for 21 days and may be renewed twice unless another patron has placed a hold. DVDs are loaned for 7 days and cannot be renewed. Late fees are 10 cents per day for books, with a maximum of 5 dollars per item. Study rooms can be reserved for up to 2 hours per day, and reservations open 7 days in advance. The library is closed on Sundays and on public holidays. Free printing is limited to 20 pages per week for each cardholder.
